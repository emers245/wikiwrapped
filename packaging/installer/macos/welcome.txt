Wikipedia Wrapped turns your Wikipedia reading history into a dashboard and a map, on your own computer.

This installs it for you only, without asking for an administrator password:

  - the program, in the WikipediaWrapped-App folder of your Library folder (about 2 GB)
  - a "Wikipedia Wrapped" app in the Applications folder of your home folder

Your history and settings are kept separately, in Library/Application Support/WikipediaWrapped, and installing, upgrading or uninstalling never changes them.

Upgrading: quit Wikipedia Wrapped, then run the newer installer. It replaces the old version.
